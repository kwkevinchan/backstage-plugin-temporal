The graph draws the frame of an expanded signal or update group in place of
the signal or update that started it: the signal or update is no longer a
box of its own next to the frame, the arrows into it go on to the first
steps of the group, and the button in the frame's title bar selects it, with
the frame shown as selected. While the group is collapsed or shown as
labels, the signal or update is a box of its own as before; so it is when
it is a step of a group, started more than one group, has no arrow into its
group, or is not in the run. The timeline, the Steps tab and the Event
Groups tab are unchanged.
